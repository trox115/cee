# Synthetic example: an open decision and an unverified task

Original: "The parser change is implemented. Should it be enabled for archived files? A follow-up may be needed."

Unsupported rewrite: "The parser change is deployed and archived files must use it. The follow-up task does not exist."

Appropriate rewrite: "The parser change is implemented. Deployment is unverified. Open: whether to enable it for archived files. A follow-up may be needed; its task status is unknown."

Implemented does not mean deployed. An option is not a requirement. Missing task evidence does not establish absence, and REPLY-003 does not authorize inventing a task. Preserve an inherited error's evidence limits without automatically assigning a transformation-specific violation.
