# CEE v0.3 release-candidate decisions

Status: `0.3.0-rc.1`, local experimental release candidate. These decisions clarify application boundaries; they do not establish a measured quality improvement. Independent human incident validation remains incomplete.

| Decision | Rule scope | Rationale and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| N01 | CORE-003, CORE-009 | Preserve materially relevant behavior, conditions and literals. Reversing a predicate is material; omitting an unneeded name is not automatically a violation. Correcting a refuted source assertion is allowed. |
| N02 | CORE-002, CORE-007, AI-007 | Separate truth, deciding evidence and transformation history. Inherited errors retain absolute duties, but unchanged text alone does not establish a conversion or new preservation loss. |
| N03 | REV-003, REV-013 | A review context does not make every assertion a finding. A confirmed label needs evidential support; a false trigger on a real defect does not automatically establish every classification failure. |
| N04 | REV-012 | Keep checking conduct separate from observable evidence-gap disclosure. A conclusion alone does not establish whether verification happened. Incomplete searches do not establish absence. |
| N05 | REV-005, CORE-003, CORE-007 | Keep known-safe-fix recommendations conditional. Do not infer an unspecified patch's behavior. Preserve action modality and material unresolved questions. |
| N06 | REV-007, REV-010, REV-014 | Independently justify overlapping violations. Correct a false extension of a real defect; omit a finding with no supported defect or credible risk. Several rules can concern one incident. |
| N07 | REV-011, REV-013, REV-015, REPLY-001–003 | Clarify proposed-removal conditions, reachable defects, unknown impact and verified reply state. Missing task evidence is not proof that no task exists. |
| N08 | CORE-008, CORE-010, REV-008 | Correct, shorten, then recheck. Necessary detail takes precedence over length. No hard word-count threshold or comprehension benefit is established. |

The normative text is in [CORE](../spec/core.md), [REV](../spec/reviews.md), [AI](../spec/agents.md), [replies](../spec/replies.md) and [taxonomy](../spec/taxonomy.md). Examples are [preservation and applicability](../examples/reviews/preservation-and-applicability.md), [impact and scope](../examples/reviews/impact-and-scope.md), and [reply evidence and modality](../examples/replies/evidence-and-modality.md). Examples are fresh synthetic illustrations, not benchmark outcomes.

## Compatibility and normative scope

Existing rule IDs and levels remain unchanged. REV-011 retains SHOULD while making its proposed-removal exception explicit. The explanatory notes resolve interpretive boundaries and may change future judgments; they are not a promise of purely editorial equivalence.

REV-015 was unused. It indexes the existing unnumbered requirement that severity be justified by impact rather than intensity or confidence, at its existing MUST strength. It does not add a new severity policy. The full specification catalog grows from 37 to 38 identifiers. The runner's existing review-discussion selection grows from 27 to 28 entries; its author-reply selection remains 17. These are profile inventories, not declarations that every rule applies to every item. The portable AI module contains additional reporting duties beyond the runner's selected AI-007 entry.

No runner, response schema, scoring formula, dictionary or historical benchmark changes accompany this candidate. Historical records remain bound to their old specification/version/profile. Reproduce them with their pinned specification and runner, not by silently interpreting them against this working tree. No old score validates this release candidate.

The [proposed validation plan](v0.3-validation-plan.md) separates deterministic consistency from semantic evidence. Reviewer-to-reviewer scope, repository merge policy and new semantic scoring infrastructure remain outside this change.
